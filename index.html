What makes this a digital twin?

Connect real-world data to the virtual model

Real-world inputs

Bin fill sensors, GPS locations, vehicle position, collection records, and computer-vision classification results.

Virtual state

Each bin, vehicle, route, and zone has a digital record that updates when new events arrive.

Simulation

Test “what if” scenarios such as adding a truck, changing a pickup schedule, or moving a collection point.

Feedback loop

Compare predicted outcomes with actual collection data and improve route and segregation recommendations.

Recommended actions

Ranked by estimated impact
01 · Prevent overflow High

Prioritise bins above 80%

Schedule high-fill bins before standard stops. Expected benefit: fewer missed pickups and cleaner public spaces.

02 · Improve recovery Medium

Target contamination hotspots

Use classification trends to identify zones with mixed waste in recycling bins and deliver focused signage or awareness.

03 · Reduce travel Medium

Bundle nearby urgent stops

Group urgent bins by location and vehicle capacity before dispatching a route. Validate suggestions against live traffic.

04 · Close the loop Ongoing

Measure outcomes

Compare recommendations with actual pickup time, overflow reports, recycling recovery, and kilometres travelled.
